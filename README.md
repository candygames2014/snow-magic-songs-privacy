PRIVACY POLICY — SNOW MAGIC SONGS

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Snow Magic Songs is a mobile music game published by Wisimme. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android app, including through its advertising service.

App identifier: com.snow.magic.songs.touch

1. INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to play.

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. Please do not send sensitive personal information or personal information about a child unless necessary to address your request.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. The game does not upload these local save files to a developer-operated server.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app’s storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app.

3. ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY DATA PROCESSING

Snow Magic Songs uses Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display banner and interstitial advertisements.

Google’s advertising SDK may process technical information, such as:

• IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
• App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps, impressions, and video views.
• Diagnostic information about app and advertising performance.
• Device or app-related identifiers, where permitted by the app’s configuration and applicable platform restrictions.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, maintain service performance, and prevent fraud or abuse.

The app requests non-personalized advertisements. Its Android manifest excludes the Google advertising ID permission.

Google’s processing is described in its Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Further information about Google advertising technologies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Snow Magic Songs is intended for a general audience, including children.

The app does not ask children to create accounts or submit names, email addresses, photographs, or other personal information.

Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment and users under the age of consent. The app requests non-personalized advertisements and applies Google’s G advertising content rating filter.

These settings restrict advertising behavior but do not mean that the advertising service processes no technical information.

Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or concerns about their child’s privacy.

5. SHARING AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

The advertising service may receive information directly from the app as described above. Google may process information on servers located outside your country, subject to its privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

If you follow a link in an advertisement, the destination website or app has its own privacy practices.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is removed or overwritten.

Support messages are retained only as needed to respond to your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you have provided directly.

Google determines retention of information processed through its services under its applicable policies. Deleting local game data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google.

7. SECURITY

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. Contact us to make a request concerning information held by Wisimme.

For information processed by Google, consult Google’s Privacy Policy and available privacy controls.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or its data practices. The latest version will display an updated date.
